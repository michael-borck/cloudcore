# CloudCore Networks - Lecturer Guide

## What is CloudCore?

CloudCore Networks is an educational simulation of a cloud services company. Students interact with the site to gather information for assignments - reviewing policies, analysing documents, and interviewing AI employees (chatbots).

**Live site:** https://cloudcore.eduserver.au

---

## What Can Be Customised?

You can tailor CloudCore for your unit's specific learning objectives:

| Customisation | Example |
|---------------|---------|
| **Content Access** | Show only policies and org charts (hide technical logs) |
| **Timed Release** | Unlock interviews in Week 5, incident reports in Week 9 |
| **Employee Access** | Allow access to HR Manager but not the CISO |
| **Unit Password** | Set a password students use to access protected content |
| **Business Hours** | Chatbots only available 7am-7pm weekdays (simulates real office) |

## Access Levels

Content is released in two phases:

| Level | Typical Timing | Access |
|-------|----------------|--------|
| **Consultant** | Week 2-3 | Policies, org charts, general documentation |
| **Auditor** | Week 8-9 | Everything: interviews, logs, technical details, chatbots |

You can customise:
- When each level unlocks
- What content is included in each level
- Which employees are available to interview

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When will my changes go live?
Typically within 1-2 business days of your request.

### Can I change things mid-semester?
Yes, but give at least 48 hours notice. Avoid changes during assignment submission periods.

### What if students share the password?
The password is per-unit, not per-student. This is by design - focus is on learning, not security. If you need individual tracking, let me know.

### Why can't students access chatbots at 2am?
Simulates realistic business hours. If a student's assignment is due at 9am, they can't interview the CEO at 3am the night before. This encourages planning and time management.

### Can I see what students are accessing?
Currently no real-time analytics. If you need evidence of engagement for assessment, students can screenshot their chat sessions or include interview transcripts in submissions.

### What if a chatbot gives wrong information?
The AI employees are based on small language models and may occasionally give inconsistent answers. This is acknowledged to students and can be a learning opportunity - in real consulting, you'd seek clarification or a second opinion.

### Can I add my own content?
Yes! You can request:
- Custom policy documents
- Additional employee profiles
- Specific scenario details

Send the content and I'll add it to the site.
